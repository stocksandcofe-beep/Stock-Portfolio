Max Drawdown ? Maximum Drawdown (MDD) is a key financial risk metric measuring the largest percentage drop from a portfolio's highest value (peak) to its lowest value (trough) before a new peak is attained. It represents the maximum cumulative loss an investor could have experienced, helping to assess downside risk and volatility.

Beta ? Measures an asset's systematic risk or volatility compared to the overall market (usually the S&P 500). Indicates how much a stock's price is expected to move relative to market shifts.
